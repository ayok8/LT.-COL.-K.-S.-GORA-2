Executive Officer Profile

Committed to Sovereign Integrity and Tactical Superiority

Lieutenant Colonel K. S. Gora is a senior commissioned officer of the Nigerian Army with a distinguished record of service spanning tactical troop leadership, operational intelligence analysis, and high-level joint military operations.

Educated through elite military institutions including the prestigious Nigerian Defence Academy (NDA) and Armed Forces Command and Staff College (AFCSC) Jaji, Lt. Col. Gora has consistently demonstrated strategic leadership in complex asymmetrical warfare, peace support missions, and multinational defense collaborations.

His career reflects an unwavering commitment to operational readiness, soldier welfare, dynamic intelligence-led security planning, and fostering international defense bilateral ties.
